# FE-TRAINING-010 – Bulk Training Actions and Print

Select persisted trainings individually across dates and filters; the selected count includes hidden selections. The header checkbox selects or deselects all currently visible persisted trainings without changing hidden selections; it shows a partial-selection state and is disabled when no persisted training is visible. Import rows are not selectable. The **Akcje** menu and selected count appear immediately above the list only when at least one training is selected. Athletes can print only; staff can additionally export selected trainings, complete planned trainings or delete selected trainings. Completion skips and reports undated plans; other failures leave the batch unchanged. Deletion requires the irreversible warning in FE-TRAINING-005. After a successful write, clear selection and refresh; retain it on failure. JSON import is a separate list action, not a selected-items bulk action; see FE-TRAINING-011.

Printing opens a dedicated preview, never prints immediately, and fetches a fresh print-safe batch. Athletes never receive equipment or other assigned athletes' identities. Returning from the preview clears the selection and reloads the list.

Print A4 landscape with a 10 mm margin and two equal columns. Order undated first, then dates ascending, and show each date once. Print group names when multiple groups are represented and staff-visible names for individual assignments. TEMPO prints ordered definitions, distances and rest, never participants or execution results. FREE_TEXT prints all ordered sections. Merge equipment per day by splitting lines and commas, trimming and deduplicating case-insensitively; only staff may see it.

Measure actual content for pagination. Two days share a page when each fits a column. For a day with multiple trainings, move an individual training whole to the next column when it fits a full column but not the remaining space. A single-training day may continue across columns; an oversized training in a multi-training day may also continue, with a warning. Never clip or omit content; equipment follows all trainings for its day and may continue. Do not repeat dates. Browser printer margins, scaling and headers can affect output.
